Infomity Studio Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Published by Infomity

Our Core Privacy Philosophy

At Infomity, privacy is not a compliance checkbox; it is the fundamental architectural constraint of everything we engineer. We build applications and developer tools that run entirely on the user's hardware with zero telemetry, zero background trackers, and zero server-side surveillance.

1. Zero Data Harvesting

Infomity does not sell, rent, monetize, or broker user information. Our software products (including mobile applications, system utilities, and open-source repositories) operate strictly without user telemetry, device profiling, advertising networks, or third-party behavioral analytics.
